Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: September 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Factavelo (“Factavelo,” “we,” “us” or “our”) handles information when people visit this website, communicate with us or authorize a supported social-media integration. It also explains the choices available to users.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the Factavelo website, official social profiles and web-based tools used to manage Factavelo's authorized social-media accounts. A third-party platform's own privacy policy also applies when you use that platform.

2. Information we may process

  • Information you provide: messages, requests and information you voluntarily send when contacting Factavelo.
  • Website information: basic technical and security information that may be recorded by our hosting provider, such as request time, browser type and IP address.
  • Authorized platform data: account identifiers, display/profile information, access authorization, publishing status, video identifiers and video statistics made available through an official API.
  • Content records: captions, media references, publication times and operational logs needed to publish or manage Factavelo content.

We do not request TikTok passwords. Authentication occurs through TikTok's authorization process.

3. TikTok data and permissions

When the Factavelo TikTok account authorizes our integration, we use approved permissions only to authenticate the account, retrieve basic account information, publish Factavelo content, check publication status, and retrieve information and engagement statistics for Factavelo videos. We do not use the integration to access private messages, contacts or unrelated accounts.

4. How we use information

We use information to operate and secure the website; authenticate authorized accounts; prepare, publish and manage educational content; record publishing results; measure Factavelo video performance; respond to requests; troubleshoot errors; maintain compliance; and improve our content and services.

5. Legal grounds

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we rely on consent for platform authorization, legitimate interests in operating and securing Factavelo, performance of requested services, and compliance with legal obligations. Consent may be withdrawn without affecting earlier lawful processing.

6. Sharing and service providers

We may share limited information with providers that support hosting, cloud storage, automation, analytics and social-media publishing. They receive only information reasonably required for their function and are subject to their own terms and privacy obligations. We may disclose information when legally required, to protect rights or safety, or in a legitimate organizational transfer. We do not sell or rent personal information.

7. Storage and retention

Information may be processed in other countries because service providers operate internationally. We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described here, including security, recordkeeping and legal requirements. Authorization tokens are restricted to systems that require them. Records may be deleted or anonymized when no longer needed.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information, including access restrictions and official authorization methods. No online system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or portability regarding personal information we control. You may revoke TikTok authorization through TikTok account settings. Contact us with a privacy request using the official profile below. We may need sufficient information to verify and respond to a request.

10. Children

Factavelo is not intended to collect personal information from children below the minimum age required to consent to online data processing in their country. If you believe a child provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review and, where appropriate, remove it.

11. Third-party links

Our content may link to third-party websites or platforms. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, and Factavelo is not responsible for those practices.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect operational, legal or platform changes. The effective date above identifies the latest version. Material changes will be highlighted when reasonably appropriate.

13. Contact

For privacy questions, requests or complaints, contact Factavelo through the official Factavelo TikTok profile. Please do not send passwords, access tokens or other credentials.
