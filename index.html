About Factavelo

History made clear, visual and memorable.

Factavelo is an educational content publisher. We turn carefully selected topics into concise, engaging videos designed for social platforms. Our goal is to make reliable learning approachable without removing the context that makes every story meaningful.

What we publish

Stories worth discovering.

History & civilizations

People, turning points and everyday details that shaped societies across time.

Archaeology & mysteries

Objects, discoveries and unanswered questions explained with historical context.

Science & inventions

Short explanations of discoveries and ideas that changed how people understand the world.

TikTok integration

Authorized publishing and performance reporting.

Factavelo uses TikTok's official developer services only for accounts that have expressly authorized access. The integration can authenticate the account, publish Factavelo videos and retrieve information about Factavelo's published videos, including public engagement statistics. Access is limited to the permissions required and can be revoked through TikTok.

Factavelo does not request access to private messages, contacts or unrelated TikTok accounts. We do not sell TikTok user data.
